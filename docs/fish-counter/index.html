How it works and tips
  1. Each frame is scaled down and compared with a slowly updating background model of the scene.
  2. Pixels whose color differs by more than the threshold are marked as moving. Isolated noise pixels are removed, and nearby fragments are merged.
  3. Connected groups of moving pixels are counted. Groups below the minimum size are ignored.
  4. The size-based estimate compares each group with the typical group size. Two fish swimming together count as 2 rather than 1.
  • Keep the phone fixed. Any camera movement looks like motion everywhere, and the background then resets automatically.
  • Use Select area to exclude reflections, the frame and the room outside the glass.
  • Fish that stop moving fade into the background over time, and only moving fish are counted.
  • Steady, even lighting gives the best results. Turn off pumps or bubblers if their bubbles are counted.
